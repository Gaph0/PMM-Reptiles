# Project Mamono Reptiles

Reptile mamonos for Project Mamono, for RimWorld 1.6. It adds eleven scaled
species, two realms of the mountains, and a cold-blooded body that lays eggs,
sheds its hide and sleeps through the cold.

- **Eleven reptile mamono.** Serpent-tailed hunters and talkers, dragon-kin who
  fly and feel no fire, cave tunnellers, swordmasters and one swamp brute. Each
  one has her own body, her own hide and her own place in the world.
- **Two mountain realms.** Dragonia, a neutral realm of dragons and humans that
  trades with you, and the Scalebound Broods, raiders who never make peace.
- **A reptilian body.** Tough scales, a love of heat, a cold that sends her to
  sleep instead of killing her, a single egg in place of a live birth, and a hide
  shed once a year that can be worked like leather.
- **Dragonian goods, and a vial of dark blood.** A violet metal, lamps that call
  a wild dragon, a wedding collar, a strong red wine - and blood that remakes a
  woman into a dragonewt, and a dragonewt into a malef dragon.

**Requires:** Harmony, Biotech, Project Mamono, Big and Small - Framework, Vanilla Expanded Framework

**Optional:** Ideology (the Broods' war meme, and its ruined archotech sites), Odyssey (volcanic hunting grounds for the salamander, and Odyssey's own ruins)

## Content

Eleven reptile mamono live in the mountains, caves, deserts, wetlands, rivers and
lakes of the world, and in the keeps of two realms. You meet them as raiders at
your door, as wild women you can tame, as traders who bring dragonium and wine,
and as women you take in. Every one of them butchers into the family's own meat,
and her corpse gives her own hide.

### Xenotypes

| Mamono | Lore | Purpose in game | In plain words |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Basilisk** | A venomous hunter of caves and deserts, whose strikes pass a slowing venom into her prey - alive, always alive, for what use is a dead mate to her? | A hunter of the realm's caves, and a fighter who slows a man until he cannot run. | As tough as a human and slower, because of the tail, but her venom ends any chase. |
| **Lamia** | A silver-tongued serpent of the high caves and mountains, who coils her way into trouble as easily as she talks her way out of it. | The realm's talker: a fine negotiator, recruiter and trader, and a grappler in a fight. | As tough as a human, no quicker, and very good with words. |
| **Medusa** | A rarer and stranger cousin of the lamia, dwelling among cold stones and forgotten ruins. | The one who turns people to stone, and the ambusher who waits in ruins for passing caravans. | As tough as a human and slower, but her gaze freezes you where you stand for two days. |
| **Wurm** | A colossal tunneller of the deep caves and wet places: immense strength, and almost no room left for thinking. | The realm's brute, and the heaviest hitter here. | Bigger than a human and much harder to kill, and slow to learn anything. |
| **Dragon** | A winged queen of the mountain keeps, wreathed in fire she cannot feel, and utterly certain of her own magnificence. | The realm's lady-knight: she flies, she ignores fire, and she keeps a jealous hoard. | Harder to kill than a human, flies, and no flame can hurt her - but she takes every slight badly. |
| **Lizardman** | A peerless blade-fighter of the caves, quick to anger and quicker with a sword. | The realm's soldier, and the best hand in a melee. | A little tougher than a human, heals fast, and deadly with a blade. |
| **Wyvern** | A swift cousin of the dragon, leaner and quicker on the wing, without her great strength or her insufferable pride. | A flyer: she carries a caravan over the mountains and dives on raiders. | About as tough as a human, quicker, and flies. |
| **Malef Dragon** | A dark, corrupted dragon of terrible beauty, whose teasing touch remakes the women she claims. | The Broods' worst: she flies, she unravels a man's will, and she turns women into her own kind. | As hard to kill as a dragon, flies, and the most persuasive woman here. |
| **Dragonewt** | What a woman becomes under a malef dragon's touch: newly scaled, teasing, and hungry for something she never asked for. | The Broods' fast talker, and the rank just below a malef dragon. | About as tough as a human, with claws and a fine way with words. |
| **Salamander** | A fire-kissed breed of lizardman, born in volcanic caves and rivers of cooling lava. | The Broods' fireproof fighter, at home where nothing else could stand. | About as tough as a human, and fire cannot touch her. |
| **Bunyip** | A brutish, swamp-dwelling cousin of the lamia who haunts rivers and still lakes, and would sooner drag you under than greet you. | Wild and tameable, and one of the strongest fighters here; she belongs to no realm. | Tougher than a human and strong in a close fight, and hopeless at talking. |

Bunyips are wild only. Malef dragons and dragonewts are never wild: they come
from the Broods, or from a vial of dark blood.

### Genes

The seven genes this mod adds. Every species also carries the core Mamono gene,
and several carry Project Mamono's venom, fiery body or claws.

- `reptilian` - tough scales, a body that loves heat, a single egg in place of a
  live birth, and a hide shed once a year. Sharp armour +15%, comfortable minimum
  temperature +10°C, and cold cannot kill her: it slows her, then puts her to
  sleep until she is warm again. Metabolic efficiency -1, complexity 2.
- `lamia tail` - a serpent's lower body, and the tail grapple. Metabolic
  efficiency -1, complexity 1.
- `petrifying gaze` - the gaze that freezes a victim in place. Metabolic
  efficiency -1, complexity 2.
- `malef corruption` - her blood carries the corruption, which the surgery and
  the dark blood look for. Metabolic efficiency -1, complexity 2.
- `dragon's pride` - arrogant and greedy from birth. Metabolic efficiency -2.
- `wurm's mind` - dull of thought, and half a body larger than her sisters. Body
  size +0.5.
- `wedding collar` - while a collar is worn she is neither greedy nor arrogant.
  No metabolic cost.

### Events

- **A wild mamono wanders in** - a wild reptile walks onto your map and can be
  tamed. It happens in the land she belongs to: the mountains and caves bring
  lamias, basilisks, wurms, wyverns, dragons, medusas, lizardmen and salamanders,
  the desert brings a basilisk, the wetlands a wurm, and rivers and lakes a
  bunyip. Each kind is rare, and only the right land can produce it.
- **A dragon answers the lamp** - a calling orb you have installed can draw a
  wild dragon to your colony at night, whatever the land around you is like. When
  a dragon answers, the orb goes quiet and becomes a glowing orb.
- **A medusa in the ruins** - a caravan that passes near a ruin can be ambushed.
  One medusa waits among broken walls and the stone figures of her victims, and
  the ambushers carry anyone they down away with them.
- **Dragonia comes to trade** - a dragonian caravan arrives with scales,
  dragonium, dragon orbs, wedding collars and wine for sale, and buys art. A
  visiting group from the realm can include a trader of its own.

### Abilities

- `tail grapple` - "Lash out with her tail and drag the victim into her coils.
  Constricted victims cannot get away while her teasing embrace wears their will
  down." He is pulled next to her and held for an hour, and he can try to wrench
  free every few minutes - a small chance, better if he is stronger than she is.
  The coils hold even while she carries him off, and let go if she goes down.
  Cooldown 6 hours, range 5 cells, no cost.
- `petrifying gaze` - "Fix a victim with the stone-cold gaze of the medusa. The
  target is frozen where she stands - downed, hunger and weariness held in
  perfect stasis - for two days." She wakes standing when the stone lets go. The
  gaze needs a working, uncovered eye: a blind woman, or one wearing a war mask
  or a veil, cannot be turned. Cooldown 7 days, range 10 cells, no cost.

### Factions

- **Dragonia** - a medieval mountain realm where dragons and humans live side by
  side. Neutral: it starts at peace, sends caravans and visitors that trade with
  you, and can be befriended or offended. It fights only if you make it an enemy.
- **Scalebound Broods** - lewdist raiders of the same mountains, out to claim men
  and remake women. Permanent enemies: they raid, they never trade, and they
  never make peace. Their raiders and captured camps carry vials of dark dragon's
  blood.
