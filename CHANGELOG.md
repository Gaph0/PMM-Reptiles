- 2026-09-24: Removed the Medieval Overhaul cave-snake patch and the rest of that coupling: its roster, its member kind and the ruin's symbol swap.

- 2026-09-24: Changed the ambush lord to kidnap - `canKidnap` was false, so with `canSteal` also false the ambushers had nothing to do once the caravan was downed (the owner's report) - and cut the spawn roll from 1-3 medusas to a fixed one (`AmbushMedusas`).

- 2026-09-24: Changed the ambush to match Odyssey's landmarks as well as ruin world objects: landmarks are tile mutators (`Tile.Mutators`) sharing the landmark's defName, so `LandmarkNear` walks rings out to `RuinProximityTiles` and matches nine names. `MedusaRuinFill` keeps the world-object test, so landmark maps stay undressed. Also added Ideology's `AbandonedArchotechStructures`.

- 2026-09-24: Added the gaze's eye requirement to `CompAbilityEffect_Petrify` (`Valid`, `AICanTargetNow` and `Apply`): `PawnCapacityDefOf.Sight` under 0.2, or apparel whose `bodyPartGroups` include `FullHead`/`Eyes`, the groups the human eye parts carry. Open-faced helmets are `UpperHead` and do not block it.

- 2026-09-24: Changed the coil's tease to 0.045 every 60 ticks, from 0.04 every 300. At the old rate a fresh man needed about 2.7 hours to break, so the one-hour coil could never finish him; severity now reaches the 0.9 break threshold in roughly 1150 ticks.

- 2026-09-24: Changed `Hediff_Constricted` to roll escape every 312 ticks instead of once: `10% + (man STR - momo STR) / momo STR` per roll, via the new `IsekaiCompat.Strength` (mobs fall back to the base value). A success ends the coil through the timer.

- 2026-09-24: Removed `Hediff_Constricted`'s same-map test as well: a kidnapped victim is despawned while carried, so the coil was deleted the moment she picked him up.

- 2026-09-24: Removed the adjacency requirement from `Hediff_Constricted`. A lamia who took one step lost her victim on the next health tick (18 of 2500 ticks held in the test). It ends on the timer, her death, downing, or leaving the map.

- 2026-09-23: Added `aiCanUse` to the petrify and tail-grapple defs and patched Core's raider tree with a node for each. Vanilla's `JobGiver_AICastAbility` is abstract, so Reptiles ships `PMM_Reptiles.JobGiver_AICastHostileAbility`. `hostile` alone never made the AI cast.
